# 🌐 How to Host Eureka Invoicing App Online for FREE FOREVER (সারাজীবন ফ্রি ব্যবহারের নিয়ম)

To give someone a permanent link (e.g. `https://eureka-international-billing.onrender.com`) that they can open from any mobile, tablet, or laptop from anywhere in the world 24/7 without your computer being turned on:

---

## Method 1: Host Free on Render.com (Recommended for Lifetime 24/7 Use)

1. Create a free account on **[GitHub.com](https://github.com)** (takes 1 minute).
2. Create a new repository (e.g., `eureka-invoicing`).
3. Upload/Push the files of this folder (`WEBSITE OF INVOICE`) into your GitHub repository.
4. Go to **[Render.com](https://render.com)** and create a free account (Sign in with GitHub).
5. Click **New +** ➔ **Web Service**.
6. Select your `eureka-invoicing` repository.
7. Fill the settings:
   - **Name:** `eureka-international-billing` (or any name you like)
   - **Runtime:** `Node`
   - **Build Command:** `npm install`
   - **Start Command:** `node server.js`
   - **Instance Type:** `Free`
8. Click **Deploy Web Service**!
9. In 1-2 minutes, Render will give you a permanent link:
   👉 **`https://eureka-international-billing.onrender.com`**
10. Send this link to anyone! They can use it forever from anywhere in the world!

---

## Method 2: Instant Public Link Right Now (Using START_ONLINE_LINK.bat)

If you want an instant online link right now without creating accounts:
1. Double-click the file **`START_ONLINE_LINK.bat`** on your desktop.
2. It will display a public URL (e.g. `https://funny-cat-42.loca.lt`).
3. Send that link to the other person!
*(Note: For Method 2, your computer must stay turned on while they use it.)*
